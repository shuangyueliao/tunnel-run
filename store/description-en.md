# Tunnel Run — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Steer a glowing orb down an endless neon tunnel. Thread the shifting gates, collect energy orbs, and grab shields to survive one crash. The speed keeps building — how deep can you run?

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Endless Runner / Casual
- Controls: Touch drag or mouse (mobile & desktop). Keyboard: ←/→ or A/D.
- Features: Endless procedural tunnel with gradually drifting gates, energy orbs (+1), gate pass (+1), shield power-up every 500 points (absorbs one crash), particle explosions with screen shake, speed lines and parallax stars, best-score saving, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
